Privacy Policy

Who we are

Our website address is https://ycalex.com, and our direct contact email is info@ycalex.com.

Personal data we collect and why

This website is a static informational site and does not use online contact forms or user registration. If you choose to contact us directly via email at info@ycalex.com, we will process your email address, name, and any information you voluntarily include in your message solely to respond to your inquiry and evaluate potential consultancy requests (Article 6(1)(b) and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may automatically record standard technical connection logs (such as IP addresses) strictly to ensure the security, reliability, and delivery of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising, profiling, or tracking cookies, nor does it use third-party analytics trackers. No user accounts or comment cookies are set on this site.

Embedded content and external links

Pages or articles on this site may include links to external websites (such as news sources). Clicking on external links will direct you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies and data collection practices.

How long we retain your data

Personal data received via email are retained only for as long as necessary to handle and follow up on your inquiry, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable legal, administrative, or professional obligations, or if a formal client relationship is established.

What rights you have over your data

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data, as well as the right to restrict or object to processing and the right to data portability, where applicable. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

You can exercise your rights at any time by writing to info@ycalex.com.
