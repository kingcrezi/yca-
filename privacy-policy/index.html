Privacy Policy

Who we are

Our website address is: https://ycalex.com.

Personal data we collect and why

When you use our contact form, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your first name, last name, email address, and the content of your comment or message.

We process these data solely to respond to your inquiry and evaluate potential consultancy requests. The legal basis for this processing is the performance of pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in responding to business communications (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Contact form submissions are transmitted and processed through [Form Service Provider], acting as data processor for message delivery.

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may automatically record standard technical connection logs (such as IP addresses) strictly to ensure the security, reliability, and delivery of the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising, profiling, or tracking cookies, nor does it use third-party analytics trackers. No user accounts or comment cookies are set on this site.

Embedded content and external links

Pages or articles on this site may include links to external websites (such as news sources). Clicking on external links will direct you to third-party websites that operate under their own privacy policies and data collection practices.

How long we retain your data

Personal data submitted through the contact form are retained only for as long as necessary to handle and follow up on your inquiry, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable legal, administrative, or professional obligations, or if a formal client relationship is established.

What rights you have over your data

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your personal data, as well as the right to restrict or object to processing and the right to data portability, where applicable. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority.

You can exercise your rights at any time by contacting us through our Contact Us page.
